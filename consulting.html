The approach

Start with people.
Build for the work.

Every organization has work that could be easier: a decision spread across too many documents, a process that depends on memory, or a small team juggling disconnected tools.

I explore the problem, shape a useful workflow, and build and refine the experience around the people who will use it. AI can help with research, drafting, and decision support while people remain responsible for the decisions.
