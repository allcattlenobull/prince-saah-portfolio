What could AI make easier for your team?

I’m Prince Saah. I use AI and technology to make the way businesses work faster, simpler and less manual. I collect the problems people quietly put up with, then build practical tools around them.

How I work

Start with people. Build for the work.

  1. What’s actually hard here?

    Every organization has work that could be easier: a decision spread across too many documents, a process that depends on memory, or a small team juggling disconnected tools.

  2. What would better look like?

    I explore the problem, shape a useful workflow, then build and refine the experience around the people who will use it.

  3. Who makes the call?

    AI helps with research, drafting, and decision support. People remain responsible for the decisions.
